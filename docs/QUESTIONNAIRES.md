# Questionnaire specification

## Questionnaire 1 — Virtual Patient
Scale: 1–5 — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree.

1. The virtual patient responded coherently and consistently
2. The virtual patient's responses were appropriate for the conversation
3. The virtual patient behaved in a way that felt realistic
4. The virtual patient's responses reflected the information the student provided
5. The virtual patient does not understand the emotional experience of real patients
6. The virtual patient understands the situation from the patient's perspective
7. The virtual patient understands the meaning behind what was said

Open-ended: Were there any specific issues with the virtual patient's behavior?

## Questionnaire 2 — Student
Scale: 1–10.

- Diagnosis accuracy
- Question Quality
- Clinical Reasoning
- Communication
- Time management

## Questionnaire 3 — AI Feedback
Scale: 1–5 — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree.

1. The feedback was relevant to the student's interaction with the virtual patient
2. The feedback provided helpful suggestions for improvement
3. The feedback was too general to be useful
4. The feedback did not reflect what actually happened during the interaction
5. The feedback did not understand important parts of the student's and the virtual patient's interaction
6. The feedback reflected the key aspects of the student's interaction with the virtual patient
